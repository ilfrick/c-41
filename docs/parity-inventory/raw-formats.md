# S9 inventory — RAW, sensors, formats, codecs

**Comparison pins:** darktable upstream `97912518a5a9a9786aeee3c38116fbc999412ff8`; C-41 `d908a71bb8345f69f07208ffd92ff88ff2266f47`. This is a source-path inventory at those revisions, not a claim of exhaustive commit-by-commit or camera-by-camera parity. Status means the evidence listed here; uncertain behavior is explicitly left for verification.

## Method and counts

- Confirmed the pinned refs and inspected upstream RAW dispatch/format tables (`src/imageio/imageio.c`, `src/imageio/imageio_rawspeed.c`, `src/imageio/imageio_libraw.c`), processing (`src/iop/rawprepare.c`, `demosaic.c`, `highlights.c`, DNG opcode code), and format readers/writers under `src/imageio/`.
- Traced C-41's implemented paths through `crates/c41-core/src/rawimage.rs`, `iop/`, `crates/c41-ui/src/raw_preview.rs`, `dialogs/mod.rs`, `export.rs`, plus DB metadata/history. Examined tests as evidence of exercised behavior, not as proof of support for all cameras/files.
- The roadmap's pinned-baseline aggregate is **2,379 upstream-only commits and 789 C-41-only commits** (point-in-time counts, not this workstream's feature count). Relevant upstream candidates below are a selected trace, not all RAW/codec changes. No count of supported camera models/formats is asserted: extension lists and installed crate names do not establish decodability or semantic parity.

## Explicit uncertainty rule

A capability remains **uncertain** until the call path and behavior are verified end-to-end with representative fixture(s) and tests at the pinned baseline. Extension acceptance, decoder crate/camera-table entries, UI labels, FFI kernels, metadata CRUD, or a writer helper are insufficient evidence by themselves. In particular, verify X3F, HEIC/HEIF/AVIF deployed-path operation, DNG GainMap and ForwardMatrix semantics, and RAW history/redecode before marking them supported.
